# What is this?

Historical Video AI is a platform for creating immersive historical experiences through AI-assisted research, storytelling, and media generation. Its goal is to recreate historical environments from a first-person perspective, allowing viewers to experience daily life, culture, and significant events as though they were present.

The project prioritizes historical immersion over historical exposition. Rather than telling viewers about history, it aims to let them experience history.

# What is the user experience?

Viewers explore historically reconstructed environments from a first-person perspective. As they move naturally through streets, marketplaces, homes, and other locations, they encounter moments that reveal how people lived, worked, and interacted. Narration provides historical context while allowing the environment itself to tell much of the story. "What it would really feel like to live during these times" is an overall goal.

# Who is the audience?

Primary:
General audiences interested in history and education.

Secondary:
People interested in travel, archaeology, historical reconstruction, and immersive media.

# What is the first product goal?

Create a repeatable pipeline capable of consistently producing publishable historical videos and validating audience interest through real-world engagement.

# Scope

- Accept a historical topic.
- Produce research-backed content.
- Generate narration.
- Produce structured scene descriptions.
- Eventually generate videos.
- Support publishing workflows.


# Not initially in scope 

- Real-time interactive worlds.
- Fully autonomous historical research.
- Training custom AI models.
- Building a video hosting platform.
- Supporting every possible historical era.
- Perfect simulation of historical reality.
- Maximizing technical complexity over delivering a usable product.
