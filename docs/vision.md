What problem are we solving?

Historical education often relies on static text, images, or documentaries. This project explores whether AI-generated immersive experiences can make historical environments more accessible and engaging.

What does success look like?

The project is successful if it can produce engaging historical media experiences that attract real audience interest. Initial validation will come through publishing content and measuring audience engagement.

What is explicitly out of scope?

- Building a general-purpose AI video generator.
- Creating a fully autonomous AI company.
- Supporting every historical period.
- Perfect historical simulation.
